Questions before you request

Do I need to be home for delivery or pickup? +
No — just tell us where to leave the totes on the request form. Many customers have us drop off on a porch or garage and pick up the same way after they've moved out.
What if I need the totes longer than 60 days? +
Message or call us before your pickup date and we'll extend the rental at +$1 per tote for each additional 30 days.
What happens if a tote is lost or damaged? +
Normal wear is fine. If a tote is cracked, badly damaged, or not returned, the cost comes out of your refundable deposit — we'll always walk you through it before anything is withheld.
Can delivery and pickup be at two different addresses? +
Yes — that's the whole idea. Tell us your pickup (old) address and delivery (new) address on the request form, and each just needs to fall within our service area.
Do you help carry or load anything? +
We handle the totes and supplies only — drop-off and pickup. We don't provide moving labor, but a dolly or hand truck rental can make a DIY move a lot easier.
How far in advance should I book? +
A few days out is usually plenty, but the sooner you request, the more likely we can hit your exact preferred delivery date — especially on weekends.
